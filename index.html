  • Accessible archives

    Their email, calendar, files, chats and call transcripts are stored with read-only access. Cryptographically verified.

  • Secured in your cloud

    The archive lives in your company’s own Google Cloud or Microsoft Azure account, not on our servers. You own it, and you decide how long it stays and who has access to the agent.

  • Conversational interaction

    Ask the agent in plain English. Each answer names the email, message, call or file it came from, so anyone can check it.
